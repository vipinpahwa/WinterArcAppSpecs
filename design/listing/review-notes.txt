Winter Arc is an offline habit tracker for a self-imposed, time-boxed
challenge. There is no account and no sign-in, which is why "Sign-in required"
is unchecked — there is nothing to sign in to.

SEEING THE APP WITH DATA
A fresh install starts empty, so Progress has nothing to show until days have
passed. The quickest way to see it populated:

1. Tap "Set up my arc".
2. Dates — set the start date about three weeks in the past.
3. Pillars — pick any three of the presets (three is the minimum).
4. Habits — accept the suggested habits or add your own.
5. Ruleset — choose any of the three (Streaks, Consistency Score, Grace Days).
6. Why & reminder, then Before photo — both can be skipped.
7. Catch up — this step appears only because the start date is in the past.
   Choose "Mark them all done".
8. Review — tap Start Arc.

The Progress tab then shows a populated heat map, streak counts and a
consistency score. Tapping any past cell opens that day's detail.

SEEING THE END-OF-ARC FLOW
Set the device date (Settings > General > Date & Time) past the arc's end date
and reopen the app. The completion summary is presented, followed by the
closing reflection and the optional after-photo step.

PERMISSIONS — ALL OPTIONAL
- Camera: only if you choose to take a before or after photo. Declining leaves
  every other part of the app usable.
- Photo library: add-only access, requested solely when saving a before/after
  comparison image. The app cannot read the library.
- Notifications: only for the optional daily reminder.

WIDGET
Long-press the Home Screen > Add Widget > Winter Arc. The small and medium
sizes show the current day and how many habits remain.

PRIVACY
The app contains no networking code of any kind and behaves identically in
airplane mode. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
All data is stored in the app's own container and in the App Group it shares
with its widget. This matches the "Data Not Collected" App Privacy answers and
the bundled privacy manifest, which declares no tracking, no collected data
types, and one required-reason API (UserDefaults, reason 1C8F.1).
